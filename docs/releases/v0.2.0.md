# ArxivResearch v0.2.0

ArxivResearch now keeps subscriptions current by default whenever the app is available, while making fully background fetching an explicit opt-in.

## Highlights

- The app checks overdue subscriptions at launch, whenever it becomes active, after the Mac wakes, and every 60 minutes while running.
- Each subscription retains its own refresh interval; only enabled subscriptions whose last successful fetch has expired are fetched automatically.
- A new **Background automatic fetching** setting continues due checks after the app quits and cleanly unregisters the service when turned off.
- Foreground and background passes share the same due logic and recheck eligibility after acquiring a cross-process lease, preventing duplicate back-to-back fetches.
- Failed requests no longer advance the last successful fetch time, so they remain eligible for retry.
- Helper and launchd implementation details have moved into Advanced Diagnostics instead of appearing in the normal setup flow.

## Privacy

The app stores its database locally and keeps provider credentials in macOS Keychain. Abstracts or extracted paper text are sent only to providers that the user configures; Notion and Zotero receive data only when their integrations are enabled.
