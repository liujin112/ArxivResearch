# ArxivResearch v0.1.0

The first public release of ArxivResearch combines a daily paper briefing with an operations-aware research library.

## Highlights

- Daily Briefing prioritizes the most relevant new papers across subscriptions.
- Library adds research shelves, saved papers, full-row interactions, and exact per-day counts grouped by arXiv updated, published, or local added date.
- Briefing and Library remain mounted and preload analysis data in batches, removing the multi-second navigation and selection stalls from earlier builds.
- Automation now installs and verifies its launchd helper, reports health, isolates failed subscriptions, and refreshes the open app after background fetches.
- Fetch and job coordination prevents duplicate active work across repeated clicks and concurrent app/helper processes.
- Settings and activity surfaces expose provider configuration, schedule state, current work, failures, and recovery actions.
- arXiv feed and entry models declare Swift 6 `Sendable` conformance for strict-concurrency builds.

## Privacy

The app stores its database locally and keeps provider credentials in macOS Keychain. Abstracts or extracted paper text are sent only to providers that the user configures; Notion and Zotero receive data only when their integrations are enabled.
